# Known limits

- Detects monotonic **linear** trends only. Real changes can be non-linear, cyclical or caused by sensor changes. A significant trend does not identify its cause.
- Satellite records (GPM, MODIS) start around 2000, so many series are about 25 years long.
- MERRA-2 is a model reanalysis, not a direct measurement; one grid cell (~50 km) covers a city and its surroundings.
- The older loaders in `pipelines/ingest/sources.py` (POWER, earthaccess whole-file grids) have not been run against live NASA services. The West Africa grids come from `pipelines/ingest/opendap_grid.py`, which was run on 2026-11-14.
- Years-to-significance is an estimate that assumes the trend and noise continue unchanged; it uses annual means and fewer simulations than the full plan (see DECISIONS #4).
- Gridded data covers **West Africa only** (0–20°N, 20°W–20°E). It includes MERRA-2 air temperature for 1980-01 to 2025-12 on its native 0.5° × 0.625° grid, and GPM rainfall for 2000-06 to 2025-09, averaged from 0.1° to 0.5° cells. Drawn boxes, map clicks, trend maps and presets without a cached regional result use these grids. It also includes MODIS NDVI for 2000-02 to 2025-12 at 0.5° (DECISIONS #37); EVI has no grid.
- **NDVI and cloud.** NDVI keeps only good and marginal pixels, and the rainy season over the forest belt is mostly cloud: over the Kumasi box the median share of usable pixels is 5% in August and 8% in September. So 118 of 311 Kumasi NDVI months have fewer than half their pixels usable and are missing (coverage 62%), and no year has the 10 valid months the annual check needs. On the map, 1,782 of 3,200 cells get a trend; most of the 4–8°N forest belt, including the Kumasi cell, has too few usable years and is blank. Each 0.5° map cell averages 100 sampled 500 m pixels, not every pixel. The catalog has no Terra composite starting between 2025-06-26 and 2025-11-17, so the map has no data for 2025-08 to 2025-10.
- Vegetation greenness for Kumasi exists as **EVI** (MOD13A3 v061 via AppEEARS, every month except January; the export counts all 1,512 pixels in every month, so none were screened out) and as **NDVI** (MOD13A1 v061, quality-screened, rainy-season gaps). They are different indices and are not interchangeable. The EVI export has no January values, so 26 of 321 months (every January from 2001 to 2026) are missing.
- The ground-station check is not possible with the current file: station `GHM00065442` has monthly mean temperature in 7 months, 5 of which overlap MERRA-2 (`docs/VALIDATION_REPORT.md`).
- Rainfall is reported in mm/day. NASA's metadata does not confirm that the monthly value is a mean rate over the month, so it is not converted to mm/month (DECISIONS #30).
- West Africa rainfall has both significant wetting (largest cluster 32 cells, near Lake Chad) and significant drying (largest cluster 25 cells, near the Cameroon coast). The drying cluster is below the opposite-trend detector's 1% minimum (32 cells), so the API reports `found: false` and says so in numbers: "49 significant rising cells and 38 significant falling cells after false-discovery control; the largest drying area (25 cells) is below the 32-cell minimum, so no single opposite-trend region is reported." With a fixed minimum of 3, 8 or 16 cells the pair would be found (sensitivity check only, `docs/VALIDATION_REPORT.md` §4). Changing that threshold needs STATS sign-off.
- Cache-backed run IDs are reconstructed from the request and cache after restart; fixture-only legacy IDs are not available in real mode.
- Map cache builds reject gridded inputs larger than 25,000 cells or 600 monthly slices and cap derived maps at 10,000 cells. These are processing limits, not dataset resolution claims.
- Twi labels in `data/catalog/` are drafts and must be reviewed by a fluent speaker.
